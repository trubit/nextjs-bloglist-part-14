# A home for good reads

Bloglist brings blog posts and the people who recommend them together. Browse the collection, find a useful article, and keep your own reading list close at hand.

## Explore

- [Browse all blogs](/blogs) to discover posts shared by the community.
- [Meet the users](/users) and explore their public reading activity.

## Your reading list

Sign in to add other users' blogs to your personal list. New blogs you create are added automatically. Mark saved posts as read whenever you finish them.

## Get started

- [Create an account](/register) or [log in](/login).
- [Share a blog](/blogs/new) with its title, author, and URL.
- Visit your [profile](/me) to manage your API token and reading list.
